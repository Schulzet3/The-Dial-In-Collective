The Dial In Collective

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

The Dial In Collective is an espresso recipe, shot logging, and community application. This policy explains how information is handled in the app and how the owner uses a separate, private Google Drive integration for encrypted disaster-recovery backups.

Information the application handles

Depending on how you use the app, we handle account email addresses and authentication details, coffee selections and saved recipes, espresso equipment and setup preferences, shot measurements, ratings, private notes, submissions, community contributions, and technical data needed for security, synchronization, and troubleshooting. Password authentication is handled by our authentication provider; the app does not need your password in readable form.

Purposes and access

Information is used to sign users in, maintain accounts and saved coffee/shot history, synchronize devices, generate espresso recommendations, administer the private beta, investigate issues, and support data recovery. Authorized administrators may access account-associated information when necessary to operate the service. Other members are not shown account emails, private notes, or private equipment details through community features.

Community contributions

Eligible validated shots from approved coffees may contribute to community results without publishing the contributor's account identity or private notes. Removing application access does not necessarily delete previously saved records or community contributions.

Private Google Drive backup authorization

The app owner may authorize a separate automation to use Google's drive.file permission for a specifically selected backup folder in the owner's own Google Drive. This authorization is for operational backups, not for connecting the Google Drive accounts of app members.

When enabled, the automation uses Google Drive access only to create encrypted backup archives, verify their integrity and location, check for existing copies, and maintain the selected folder under the documented retention rules. It does not read unrelated Drive content, use Drive information for advertising, or sell Google user data. Encrypted archives may contain application account and shot data; archive decryption keys are kept separate from the Drive storage integration. OAuth credentials are used for the owner's authorized backup automation, not exposed to other members.

Google API information obtained for this integration is used only to provide or improve this user-facing backup functionality, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Service providers and security

The application uses Supabase for hosted data and authentication, Netlify for web hosting, and GitHub Actions and Google Drive for encrypted backup operations when enabled. These providers process information as necessary to operate their respective services. We use account access controls, encrypted connections, and encrypted offsite backup archives. No internet-connected service can promise absolute security.

Storage, retention, and requests

Account data is kept while needed to operate the service or meet legitimate security and recovery needs. Encrypted offsite backups are subject to a generally 30-day retention policy, with a minimum number of recent recovery copies preserved for safety; archived copies may persist beyond account-level changes until retention and recovery requirements permit removal. Removing beta access is not equivalent to permanently deleting account records. Requests for data access, correction, or deletion are handled subject to authentication, technical dependencies, and applicable law.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the service or backup integration changes; the effective date above will be revised. For privacy questions or requests, contact schulzet3@gmail.com.
