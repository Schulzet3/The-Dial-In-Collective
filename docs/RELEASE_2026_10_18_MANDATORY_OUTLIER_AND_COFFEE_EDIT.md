## Owner-Locked functionality

1. **Required anomalous-shot review.** The old engineering-only 4× median extraction-time and 3× ratio heuristics are replaced in production by source/identity-checked **median/MAD contextual review**. At least five relevant own shots on exact coffee/style/setup are preferred; fallback to five different qualifying Community contributors for the exact approved coffee/style. The modified Z score >3.5 is a **potential data issue signal** (general NIST statistical practice), combined with conservative practical deviation floors, not a scientific espresso quality standard or hard style/ratio/time gate. A 62-second shot against repeated 25-second comparable records may be flagged, but unconventional genuine espresso remains eligible after confirmation.
2. **No Later.** Held member-owned outlier displays a focused modal with only **Yes, It's Correct** and **Correct Shot**. ESC or clicking elsewhere does not dismiss a known online/synced hold; opening Edit Shot and closing without save returns to the required review. Resolution is bound to the owner's shot and current source revision/fingerprint; normal unflagged Log Shot → Rate → Recommendation remains uninterrupted. Offline and durable unsynced shots are never discarded/used as an excuse to lock users out; the flag reappears on reconnect/successful sync.
3. **Edit existing shots:** Existing expanded Shot History → Edit action remains available and was regression tested. Owner-only, expected-revision typed edits preserve immutable coffee/style/equipment/time provenance and revalidate personal and Community derived state. Does not replace history by rewriting old snapshots.
4. **Edit Coffee Details:** New Edit Coffee Details button in each coffee's detail section. Owner may revise their **own Pending proposal** using an optimistic revision and update the original library entry's coffee details, retaining shot associations. For a **Verified** globally shared canonical coffee, member saves a **pending source/provenance correction proposal** for later review. The canonical public record, everyone else's values and historical shots **are not silently changed**. Admin decision UI for applying such requested corrections remains a separate governed task; honest Pending status is necessary until facts are actually source-verified.
5. **Community browse:** Sanitized, admitted-member-only eligible Community Shot list with shot/contributor counts and progressive evidence labels now included in the frontend. It deliberately hides owner IDs, private notes and persistent contributor handles. **#11** still requires real account acceptance; **#12** complete contributor-weighted recommendation engine and **#10** alternate recipe families are not claimed finished.
