## Bounded verification and evidence grade
- **SUPPORTED: chemical change in tested conditions (grade A for direct measurement, not sensory evidence).** One Arabica roast batch packaged under nitrogen in 1-kg one-way-valve bags; rested three weeks before opening. Four closure/transfer treatments (integrated screw cap, clip, tape, airtight metal canister) versus unopened-reference packages; five replicate samples/treatment; 49 g removed twice weekly; GC/MS volatiles/freshness ratios plus package-headspace O2/CO2 tracked. The measured **2-butanone/2-methylfuran** index and headspace differed by handling treatment, with integrated screw cap best among these particular treatments, container/tape worse in this test. This does not rank all consumer containers or prove that a screw cap always wins.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED: tasted espresso results (grade D).** No five-input DIC taste evaluations, espresso extraction outcomes, randomized per-shot recommendation comparisons, sensory acceptance cutoff, personal roast-rest optimum or universal "stale" day. The paper explicitly requests additional sensory work before specifying consumer acceptability. No valid numeric grinder, yield, temperature or rest-day correction follows from these chemistry results.
