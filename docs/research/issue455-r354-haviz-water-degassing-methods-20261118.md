## Original method and three distinct factual claims

1. **Brew counts versus serial readings:** §2.2 says five water brands, *three independent brews each*, single 51mm DeLonghi Stilosa EC230-BK / Latina T-60, medium-dark Lampung Robusta, **12 g dose, 24 g yield, 25 s**. §2.3 states CO2 detected after brewing every **10 seconds for 120 seconds**. Table 4 CO2 ANOVA separately says **Count = 13 for each brand**, **five times 13 = 65 points**, group df 4, within df **60**, published **F = 41.21326**, **p = 1.41e-16**. Its reported ANOVA samples appear to be **longitudinal time samples**, not 13 independent physical espresso brews; the mapping of those time series onto three brew replicates is *unreported*. Consequently the statistical independence assumption and published p-value's relevance to brew-level causal water differences **cannot be established**. This is a risk/limitation, not an independent reanalysis or accusation: without raw brewer-replicate trajectories and a proper clustered/repeated-measures model, corrected inference cannot be computed. Page 3 §2.2; page 3-4 §2.3; published Table 4.
2. **Internal contradiction of the minimum:** Original abstract and §5 conclude Brand D is the lowest CO2 (about 865 ppm), but **Table 4: E = 805.1538462, D = 866.5384615 ppm**; Discussion §4.1 explicitly says Brand E is the lowest. The abstract/§5 ordering is inconsistent with the same paper's numeric table and discussion. No water ranking for DIC should adopt either as universal personal guidance.
3. **Sensory applicability:** §2.5 describes **three trained panelists** and Table 6 reports qualitative aroma/acidity/body/crema/sweetness attributes for five commercial waters with covarying mineral compositions. It does not report a randomized same-member coffee/setup/style/grinder one-control next-shot trial, all five current DIC five-point ratings, individual preference outcomes, blind/controlled mineral contrasts, or adverse effects. Water TDS, espresso beverage TDS, pH and emitted postbrew CO2 are different measurements. No source-derived personal water correction, scientific prediction confidence or improved-cup claim is established.
