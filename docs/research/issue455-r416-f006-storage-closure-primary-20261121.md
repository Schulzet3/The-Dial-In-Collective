## Evidence checks (new scoped claim receipts; do not double-count canonical ledger)

**R455-EV-R416-F006-CHEMISTRY — SOURCE SUPPORTED, bounded experiment.** One whole-bean Arabica batch after an initial three-week unopened rest; four opened-closure methods (integrated screw cap, clip, tape, transfer to canister), plus unopened reference. Five packages per method, 49 g withdrawn twice weekly for nine weeks, GC/MS aroma/headspace plus O2/CO2 measurements; not a consumer cup or espresso study. Table 1 lists **relative 2-butanone / 2-methylfuran index linear slopes per week**: canister 0.06669, tape 0.05827, clip 0.03987, screw cap 0.03269; tested pairwise slope model differences `p < 0.001` in this specific protocol. These are **not universal percentages, cup-quality rankings, aging targets or extraction coefficients**. The second reported dimethyl disulfide / methanethiol index did **not** distinguish between the four opened-closure methods. PDF pp. 2-4, Methods 2.3–2.6, Results 3.1–3.2, Table 1, Figs. 2–3.

One source and treatment design do not prove any user's packaging is chemically comparable or that one closure is universally preferable.
