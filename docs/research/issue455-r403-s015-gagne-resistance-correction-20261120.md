## Direct first-party finding and scientific limitations
- In the article's explicit [Feb. 7 correction](https://coffeeadastra.com/2021/01/16/a-study-of-espresso-puck-resistance-and-how-puck-preparation-affects-it/), the author says his original description inverted the simplified resistance relationship: he replaces **flow divided by the square root of pressure** with **square root of pressure divided by flow**. Do not propagate the original inverted description or treat a DE1 graph's derived reciprocal-style display as a validated portable pressure/flow predictor.
- Independently reread the article's actual methods and results: one experimenter, one washed Honduras coffee, Niche Zero fixed grinder setting, DE1 pressure profile, 18.0 g nominal dose, **24 espresso shots** alternating surface/deep WDT with/without a paper filter; pressure/flow/weight-derived *puck resistance* repeatability was the endpoint. The author explicitly says **he did not refract the shots** and tasted most informally, with **no blinded five-taste severity measurements, observed per-shot astringency diagnosis or individual next-cup quality trial**. Flow/wetting explanations and a proposed preinfusion pause are **hypotheses**, not experimentally identified diagnoses of dryness.
- [Cameron et al., *Matter* 2020 DOI 10.1016/j.matt.2019.12.019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matt.2019.12.019) primary full-text account establishes nonmonotonic extraction-yield versus grind at sufficiently fine settings and a plausible inhomogeneous flow mechanism **in its study conditions**; it does not record DIC's five sensory ratings or prove astringency `>=3` selects a universal puck fault. [Hoffmann's original Jan. 8, 2025 S012 creator listing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MbTD42FvMVU) shows troubleshooting chapters at 10:42 bitter and 11:27 distinguishing sour/bitter, but full video transcript/timecoded astringency claims were **NOT** independently verified.
- Evidence grade: **B for bounded physical alternative (C for S015 single-set-up experiment alone); D/UNVERIFIED for any categorical cause-from-astringency, universal threshold, extraction preference or improved next-shot five-taste result.** The February author's correction does not itself invalidate his entire observed shot series, and does not establish scientific confidence in DIC recommendation effectiveness.
