## Proposed owner-gated, voluntary prospective protocol (no activation here)

**Unit of comparison:** one willing adult member's deliberately selected baseline shot and **immediately following** shot for the exact same coffee identity, bag/lot where identifiable, espresso setup version, grinder/burr configuration and calibration, basket, independent espresso style (Turbo ≠ Fast-Flow), feed mode (slow ≠ standard), water and puck-preparation routine. Keep intended dose and any other **controllable** variable constant. No intervening shot, equipment change, different coffee release or retrospective pair selection. Original shots, five raw taste ratings, timestamps, measured grind/dose/yield/time/optional brew temperature, private notes and recipe family remain untouched. A future owner-approved mechanism would require explicit per-participant opt-in and opt-out, a plain-language description of what is stored/compared, minimum necessary purpose, retention, deletion and isolation, and no Community publication. Evaluate on pre-consented newly collected records only; never mine historical members.

**One intended control change:** record the explicit advice and chosen control before the second extraction, then record whether it was actually attempted. Eligible choices are **one of** grinder setting, beverage-yield target, or equipment-adjustable espresso brew temperature. A change in measured shot seconds can be a consequence of changing grind/yield and is **not** silently counted as a second deliberate knob; record it as an observed co-outcome. Record actual achieved variables, including unchanged intended controls and any deviation. A copied grind/temperature value is not adoption until the measured shot corroborates it. Do not label pressing two "Use Recommendation" buttons as a one-change comparison. Recipe-specific time bands are context, never universal sensory thresholds.

**Prospective sequence:** (1) member separately consents and chooses a baseline; (2) baseline is measured, timestamped and all five ratings deliberately completed, including all-green as **five real ratings**; (3) freeze the exact proposed one-control adjustment and context *before* attempting the next shot; (4) member prepares next shot while keeping other controllables fixed, recording any deviation and actual dose/yield/time/grind/temp; (5) voluntarily rate all five dimensions again; (6) pair only when fields and eligibility are independently complete. Allow refusal and abandoned attempts without penalty or hidden collection. Recipe-family changes, custom/retired settings and old calibration versions remain historically visible, but never silently translated into a measured identical-control experiment.

**Prespecified outcomes (descriptive, not causal):** retain the ordered five-dimensional before/after vectors and individual component differences. Report improved, unchanged, worsened and **mixed** outcomes separately; surface any worsening, even when other ratings move toward the existing Locked ideal. Optional descriptive count of domains nearer the current Locked ideal must be identified as a *heuristic distance*, **not** a personal liking, cup-quality, statistical-confidence, or independent sensory efficacy score. All-green at baseline versus all-green next shot is a retained success, not evidence that a recipe change was necessary or beneficial. No rating is not a rating of neutral/green. Record adverse events, member abandonment, unexpected dose changes, and self-reported undesirable taste. Retain missingness and denominator, not just successes.

**Confounders and bias ledger:** grinder drift/seasoning, freshness and opening/rest duration, coffee bag/lot, water mineral profile, machine warm-up, basket, feed mode, changing dose or puck prep, target-versus-actual yield, measured temperature versus machine set point, time of day, tasting order/temperature, participant expectation, self-selection and preference variation, edited/replayed records, community influences and offline timestamp/sync order. A before/after observational pair without randomization and controls cannot attribute perceived improvement to the app. Reporting is descriptive only until owner separately approves study methodology and actual evidence supports a stronger statement; no universal optimum or science-based confidence badge.

## Verification matrix for any future approved design (NOT EXECUTED)

| Scenario | Required expected disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Same coffee/version/basket/style/feed, baseline + next chronologically distinct, each five fully rated, exactly one intentionally changed controllable, physical measurements valid | Eligible **descriptive paired outcome**; report all five changes and uncertainties; no causal PASS assertion |
| Already all-green, then all-green; all-green and no changed control | Distinguish continuing personal success / no intervention; never manufacture a gain |
| One or more unrated taste inputs, missing valid shot time, malformed imported/archived data | Not eligible; preserve private history, mark missing reason |
| Turbo to Fast-Flow, Standard to Slow Feed, basket/dose/setup/grinder or calibration changed | Exclude as confounded; do not transfer calibration/range or merge styles |
| Copied recommended grind but typed a different value; both copied grind and temperature; unchanged target or no actual measurement | Cannot assert single-control adoption |
| Temperature cannot be controlled or original temp absent | Do not infer a temperature intervention from time/flow changes |
| Changed coffee bag/roast age/prep/water, outlier-review pending, multiple intervening shots | Exclude/flag uncertain; do not silently pool |
| Offline duplicate replay, corrected timestamp, later editing of ratings/recipe or reorder after sync | Maintain immutable observational identity and revision provenance; no fabricated sequence |
| One taste improves, another worsens; member reports adverse taste | Report mixed/worsened and adverse outcomes; never aggregate away harm |
| Original successful custom/retired family or historical grinder range | Preserve unchanged private archive; only assess a new comparison with truly compatible live context, if approved |
