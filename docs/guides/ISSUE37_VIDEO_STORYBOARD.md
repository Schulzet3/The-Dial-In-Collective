## Full caption transcript — short form

Welcome to The Dial In Collective. Here's your first espresso shot in six steps.

One: Create an account or sign in. After email verification, use your one-time invitation when prompted.

Two: Set up your workspace. Enter your setup name, basket dose, and the finest and coarsest grinder settings you use for espresso.

Three: Add a coffee. Enter its name and roaster. You can autofill from an official product link, but always review the suggested details.

Four: Choose a shot style and tap Log First Shot or Log A Shot.

Five: Enter the dose, yield, time, and grind setting you actually measured. Rate all five taste scales.

Six: Save Shot & Get Suggestion. Your coffee opens with next-shot feedback. Try the recommendation or your own adjustment, then log another shot.

Your history is always there to review. For help, contact support@dialincollective.com.
