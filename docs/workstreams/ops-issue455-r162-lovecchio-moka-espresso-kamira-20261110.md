## Newly reviewed original research and limitations
- **Primary original:** Giovanna Lo Vecchio et al. **Chemical and Sensory Differences Among Coffee Beverages Brewed by Traditional Moka, Espresso, and Kamira, a Novel Italian Brewing Device**, *Separations* 13(6):166, published 2026-06-01, DOI [10.3390/separations13060166](https://doi.org/10.3390/separations13060166); [original full publisher HTML](https://www.mdpi.com/2297-8739/13/6/166). Original Methods §2.1, chemical Results and Tables 2/4, sensory §3.8/Table 5, Discussion, Funding, Data Availability and Conflicts inspected on 2026-11-10. Original 22-page **PDF binary/figures/supplement and raw individual brew/panel data NOT downloaded or hashed**; source bytes remain unverified.
- One commercial ground blend **Lavazza Crema e Gusto** (Arabica+Robusta) and one bottled water used across brewing systems; moka about **12 g/90 mL**, DeLonghi Dedica about **7 g/25 mL/10 seconds** with **nominal pump pressure 15 bar** (NOT measured at puck), Kamira about **8 g/20 mL**. Grinder, brew ratio, extraction time, device and hydraulics are not independently controlled, so comparisons are not a controlled single espresso variable intervention.
- For each system multiple coffees were **pooled to ~400 mL** before chemical measurements; reported triplicates are within-composite analytical repeats, not independent brew-to-brew replicates or longitudinal personal outcome studies. Table 2 total solids (g/100g) moka **2.34±0.08**, espresso **2.61±0.11**, Kamira **2.02±0.09**. Table 4 caffeine mg/100g moka **41.7±2.9**, espresso **55.3±4.1**, Kamira **39.8±2.7**. These are **study conditions**, not DIC targets.
- Ten familiarized panelists evaluated 13 attributes twice; do **not** infer DIC's five subjective sliders from group sample means. Table 5 Friedman p-values: sweetness **0.032258** (Holm pairwise moka/espresso **0.039**, moka/Kamira **0.048**), acidity **0.973684**, bitterness **0.696902**, body **0.214381**, aroma intensity **0.606531**, clean cup **0.189724**. Small descriptive method comparisons and 3 pooled composition means do not establish chemical→personal sweetness prediction, pressure optimum, 10-second shot ideal, or demonstrably superior home espresso.
- Authors report no external funding; **Kamira s.r.l. supplied equipment and technical guidance**; Nicola Cicero disclosed **Science4life consultancy**, other authors denied commercial relations. Manufacturer involvement is not proof of invalidity but merits provenance marking. Raw data said to be available on request, **not acquired**. Evidence grade: peer-reviewed original descriptive one-blend/three-device design, **low causal transfer to personal espresso recommendations**.
