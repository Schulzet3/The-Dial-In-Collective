## Three bounded checks, not taste-efficacy proof
1. **Methods:** 77 university-associated adults, six milk/sugar-adjusted Nespresso capsule café lattes (0/2.5/5% sugar; 1:2 or 1:3 espresso:milk), fixed real café → booth → mixed-reality order despite within-context randomized sample order. Confounds context with period/experience. No randomized espresso shot adjustment.
2. **Ratings/results:** 9-point hedonic liking, 25-term CATA and Ideal CATA, not DIC's five 1–5 sensory scores. No significant overall liking contrast between within-study analytic/holistic groups; some sample-context differences; small context eta squared .036. RV≈.88 is attribute-space agreement, not personal flavor prediction or improved cup quality.
3. **External validity/privacy:** Authors say cross-context results are noncausal due fixed order; university cohort, relative analytic/holistic split with alpha .66 and raw data privacy restrictions also limit transfer. The work cannot justify universal taste targets, a confidence percentage, new member-context telemetry, a new espresso engine correction or a production experiment.
