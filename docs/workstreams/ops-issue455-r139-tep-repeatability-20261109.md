**Methods and exact limits:** 46 industry experts across France 7, India 12, Italy 7, Korea 10, USA 10; eight samples rated on three repetitions (138 participant-session evaluations), local roast and espresso preparation; local baristas and commercial machines/grinders with temperature/pressure checks. Roasts/recipes/water and equipment differ by country (3–10 days after roasting generally versus 20 days in India); drink served 70–90 mL per tasting in this protocol, not a recommended DIC mass-yield. Revised TEP uses CATA sensory lexicon terms plus 100-point quality questions. **Not DIC's five 1–5 original taste inputs** and not a controlled same-coffee user intervention.

**Results with uncertainty:** authors report within-person repeated CATA response grouping USA 9/10, Italy 5/7, Korea 10/10, India 9/12 and France 6/7, global homogeneity index 0.364 across 138 sessions; **only 5/10** country-pair sample-configuration RV comparisons significant; US group's eight quality means were not significantly different, and country x sample quality interaction p<0.0001. Author acknowledges unequal small country samples, unfamiliar sensory terms, contextual roast/prep/water variation and needed terminology training. These descriptive repeatability results do not prove user preference accuracy, numeric all-green truth, algorithm confidence, causal grind/yield/temperature treatment or real espresso quality improvement. Source strengths: primary peer-reviewed expert espresso experiment; weakness: not novice consumers nor identical interventions, unpublished participant data, not reanalyzed.
