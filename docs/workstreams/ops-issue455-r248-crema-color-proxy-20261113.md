## Primary paper and original review

Directly read original §§1–5, §§4.1–4.4.4, conclusions, Appendices A–C, ethics/data/funding. This is an engineering *crema image-segmentation* proof-of-concept, not a human taste or personalized-recipe efficacy study. Source used nominal medium roast Agtron 55, 10-day rest, 60 mL cup, iPhone 6 images near 1150 ±10% lux, resized to 500×500, GrabCut/transparent filtering/K-means RGB clustering on laptop or Jetson Nano. Camera angles, reflections and cup geometry were acknowledged limitations.

In §4.4.2, **71%–97%** across cups and **97% in the round glass** mean proportion classified as brown *crema area* in the processed image. They are NOT percentage of sensory classifications correct. Reported 96.7%/97.5% valid crema area at two lighting conditions (§4.2) also is color-region measurement, not blind taste validation. §§4.3–4.4.4 use roast Agtron tiles and RGB thresholds to label nominal under/normal/over, and discuss generic machine pressures/shot times; **no independently labeled multi-bean espresso chemical-extraction accuracy, blinded personal five-rating panel, randomized single-change next-shot recommendation study, or improved-cup endpoint demonstrated**.
