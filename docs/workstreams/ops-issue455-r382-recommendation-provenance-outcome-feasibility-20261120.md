## Direct current-behavior finding — source provenance, not verified action/outcome
- In Log Shot, new-shot `Use Recommendation` invokes `recordRecommendationUse()` only for manually *copied* grind/temperature. `reconcileRecommendationUse()` subsequently drops those copy markers when the member edits the value; its context is `member_selected_starting_reference` with source/controls and optional descriptive Community aggregate metadata. **That proves at most an app copy event matching the saved measured field**, not that the app proposed a controlled trial, the member actually changed one variable relative to their previous pull, or the next cup tasted better.
- `shotPayload()` writes five taste ratings, measured dose/yield/time/grind, optional actual temperature, coffee/setup-version/calibration/style/basket/feed, timestamp, private notes and optional `recommendation_context`. `validateShot()` demands all five integer taste entries for a newly saved shot. `scales()` offers all five rating groups, and historical/incomplete imported rows retain their original readings. The optional provenance does **not** constitute a stable prior-shot ID, recommendation→next-shot pairing, consent, or evidence of controlled same-coffee/setup transitions.

The owner-Locked engine still limits actual algorithmic next-step advice to a controllable change, preserves green success versus missing ratings, original measurements/history/notes, separate successful families and styles, grinder calibration/feed/temperature capabilities. 1. **Permission before acquisition:** a NEW explicit owner decision, per-participant informed opt-in and revocable research consent, retention/minimization/access review with #96, and separate security/release authority would precede *any* new member-data reads, export, telemetry, instrument, UI field, SQL or provider write. Existing private shots are NOT retroactively an authorized research sample. Research-only local synthetic fixtures are permitted; no real-account experiment now.
2. **Pairing unit (candidate, not claimed effective):** original shot A → displayed advice and chosen single controllable field → next chronological shot B. Require same **verified coffee release identity**, owner/setup-version, basket, style (Turbo and Fast-Flow independent), original grinder and calibration/range, feed mode, temperature capability and recorded relevant equipment controls. Retain A and B as distinct immutable shots. Pair by explicit consented, source-verifiable causal linkage; mere chronological adjacency or matched coffee names do not prove recommendation acceptance.
3. **Single-change gate:** preserve actual A/B dose, yield, grind, temperature; verify **exactly one** intentional comparable controlled measurement changed in the recommendation's suggested direction, within recorded physical bounds. Different grinder scale/version without a validated translation, multiple simultaneous changes, edited original history, unknown feed, alternate basket or same-name different release → **noncomparable / excluded from effect estimate**, retained only as private historical data. Time is a dependent process observation, **not automatically a controllable intervention**. No universal grams/seconds/grinder-tick response threshold is asserted.
4. **Outcome:** compare the *ordered vector of five complete sensory ratings* before/after, all-green vs not-all-green, per-dimension better/unchanged/worse against each taste's owner-approved direction, and adverse/worsening dimensions. **Do not collapse into unapproved scalar taste utility or assign calibrated confidence.** Same-person repeatability, subjective preferences, multiple successful ratio families, coffee freshness/rest, puck prep, water, ambient/machine stability and temporal carryover are measured/declared confounders, not assumed controlled. One better cup ≠ proven causal improvement.
5. **Missingness and adverse outcomes:** missing/invalid ratings, saved-but-never-accepted advice, abandoned/offline-unsynced pairing, out-of-order or ambiguous timestamps, manually altered proposed values, and changed setup must be counted transparently in eligibility denominators and separately as non-evaluable, not silent success. Record worsening on any of all five dimensions, next-shot no benefit, nonadherence and participant withdrawal. Preserve original private notes and history without copying to research exports by default.
6. **Evaluation plan only, not power/benefit claim:** preregister defined outcomes, confounders, adverse thresholds, clustered within-user/coffee sessions, separate training from prospective held-out assessment, missingness handling and data minimization; compare against the existing deterministic advice baseline. No numerical optima, success probabilities, calibrated confidence intervals, superiority statistics, group sample-size or new control is scientifically justified before actual authorized observations and a scoped review.
