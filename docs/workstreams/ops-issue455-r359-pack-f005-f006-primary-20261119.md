## F005 / S003 — roasted-coffee degassing
- Original pack claim: "CO2 release from whole and ground roasted coffee varies with roast and storage conditions." Pack proposes recording coffee age where available, no universal rest day. Source: Smrke et al., *Time-Resolved Gravimetric Method To Assess Degassing of Roasted Coffee*, J Agric Food Chem **66** (2018) 5293–5300, DOI [10.1021/acs.jafc.7b03310](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jafc.7b03310), first published online 2017-11-15. Checked original ACS article's publisher-indexed abstract, methods/results/conclusion and independent [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29091435/); original publisher PDF bytes/figure reproduction not obtained.
- **Supported, bounded (instrumental chemistry; no human sensory study):** gravimetric mass-loss kinetics vary with roast degree, roast speed, whole versus ground coffee and elapsed time. Nine whole-Arabica roast profiles were measured for at least 400 hours; Arabica/Robusta ground samples were also investigated. Ground-coffee behavior differs substantially from whole beans. The gravimeter directly measures **mass of evolved gases, not selectively CO2**; the article expressly distinguishes its method from CO2-specific measurements. An uncontrolled "CO2 concentration" numerical inference from the gravimetric output would be incorrect.
- **Overextension corrected:** "storage conditions" is not an experimentally randomized packaging/storage-method comparison in this paper: semiclosed capillary conditions and 35 C were maintained for measurements. It does **not** identify a taste optimum, rest-day window, universal age-to-grind correction or a member's sensory outcome. Evidence grade: high for measured lab gas mass loss under protocol, **insufficient for quantitative DIC recommendation policy or sensory efficacy**.

## F006 / S004 — open-package storage
- Original pack claim: "Opened-coffee package closure affects chemical freshness loss and aroma indices." Smrke et al., *Effects of different coffee storage methods on coffee freshness after opening of packages*, Food Packaging and Shelf Life **33** (2022) 100893, DOI [10.1016/j.fpsl.2022.100893](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fpsl.2022.100893). Inspected full author-institution [open-access published article PDF](https://digitalcollection.zhaw.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/78b612ba-7578-4046-a3dd-4be48a182fcd/content), including visually checked PDF pages 2 and 4 (methods Fig.1; measured-index Figs.2–3), and Conclusion/Funding on printed p.6. External PDF byte SHA not independently measured.
- **Supported, bounded:** one Tchibo-provided Arabica roast batch in 1-kg nitrogen-flushed bags, rested three weeks before opening; five packages per handling condition; 49 g sampled twice weekly under room conditions. Reclosing by integrated screw cap, clip, tape or decanting into an airtight metal canister was compared against unopened references. Headspace O2/CO2 and GC/MS chemical aroma/freshness indices were measured. Under these experimental conditions, integrated screw cap preserved the **chemical** 2-butanone/2-methylfuran freshness proxy better than alternatives. Industry-cofunded by Tchibo and ZHAW, disclosed by authors.
- **Not supported:** no blinded espresso tasting, no verified member five-rating benefit, no minimum/maximum freshness lifetime, no universal advice that a particular home container will always worsen actual cups. Authors explicitly require separate sensory studies to determine consumer acceptance thresholds. Pack's S004 date `2022-09-01` should not be called the verified online publication date; original paper states available online **2022-06-24** (received 2020-07-05, accepted 2022-06-11). Evidence grade: good **single-coffee chemical/packaging** comparison, **not** a DIC sensory efficacy or general consumer-outcome estimate.
- Current DIC: Log Shot five tastes, same-coffee evidence and one-controllable-change requirement reviewed in engine/model; no current proven behavioral gap from F006. Prototype proposal "note storage only when known" is not approval for capturing a new field, telemetry or forcing a grind change.
