## Three independently checked claims and limitations
1. Three ground-size categories and 10 trained descriptive assessors were reported. The study does **not** test DIC's personal five-slider next-shot optimization across many coffees.
2. Finer condition in those categories was associated with stronger extraction and physical indices (abstract reports p=0.001), but magnitudes and confounders need full methods/table review; do not infer a global grinder setting or causal confidence estimate.
3. The fine condition received stronger flavor/crema and a favorable sweetness-acid-bitter description *within that panel*. A tiger-skin crema visual is neither a universal quality score nor evidence that every user should grind finer.
