## New nonduplicate claim checks, provenance and uncertainty
1. **R384-04-DE1-CONTROL-MODE:** Independently read the actual manufacturer's living [DE1 manual](https://decentespresso.com/docs/de1_manual) sections 4.1/4.2 (pump by pressure vs pump by flow, water-above-puck vs group sensor, calibration) and original [DE1 application quickstart](https://decentespresso.hk/docs/de1_app_quickstart_guide) Flow/Pressure profile subsections. For a pressure step, pressure is commanded and flow responds; a flow step commands water flow and permits a **maximum pressure cap that reduces flow**. The app may separately show **water entering puck** versus **coffee entering cup**; these are not the same measured control channel. The manufacturer explicitly acknowledges voltage, pump, calibration-basket and flow-estimation errors (including its one worked `~0.2 mL/s` discrepancy at range extremes), so the report's wording that the DE1 universally maintains actual `puck outflow` is **OVER-PRECISE / NOT INDEPENDENTLY ESTABLISHED**. It does implement flow-oriented hydraulic control; there is no direct published evidence in these manuals of continuous locally resolved pore/outflow feedback or zero channeling. Grade **HIGH manufacturer-operational documentation** for available settings/limit semantics; **LOW** for independent calibration reliability across machines, channel prevention, and five-taste espresso outcome superiority. Live HTML is mutable/unversioned; exact third-party page-byte SHA not obtained. No machine/scale authenticated hardware measurement.
