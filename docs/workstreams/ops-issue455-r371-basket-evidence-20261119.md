## Newly inspected sources (source pages are mutable; full web-page byte hashes NOT collected)
1. **BH-2023-BASKET-1**, Barista Hustle, *Battle of the Baskets*, published **2023-09-07**, direct first-party experiment report, https://www.baristahustle.com/battle-of-the-baskets/ , §§High Speed, High Extraction / Unexpected Resistance. Access: full public original article read Nov 19. **Evidence grade: low-to-moderate for one setup's extraction difference; not blinded sensory evidence and not universally transferable**. Original comparison held dose 16.2g but **changed grinder setting** (140 vs 172 µm), yield (44.3 vs 43.2g) and time (18.3 vs 19.0s) between Sworks Billet and IMS on a Dalla Corte Mina / Mazzer ZM / one 3065 blend in a fast-extraction regime. Reported TDS 7.9% vs 7.0%, EY 21.9% vs 19.1%, and t-test p=.016. The page does not provide enough trial-count, sampling or raw-data detail here to certify a universal effect. Unblinded “tasted great” narration is **not** a five-rating improvement experiment. The observed result is for a **redialed recipe plus basket**, not the causal effect of swapping only the basket. Hole geometry, clogging and edge-flow mechanism remain hypotheses.
2. **BH-2023-BASKET-2**, Barista Hustle, *Round Two — Battle of the Baskets*, published **2023-10-03**, direct first-party hole-image/practical methods, https://www.baristahustle.com/round-two-battle-of-the-baskets/ , §§Paradox of Modern Baskets / Exploring Basket Quality. Public original read Nov 19. **Evidence: measured basket-hole image areas, with unvalidated mechanism**: Sworks reported about 4% open area, Pesado about 14%, and both were associated with fast flow despite dissimilar designs. Article explains why empty-basket resistance alone need not dominate puck+metal series resistance, and explicitly states causal effect of differing hole spacing on extraction **remains undetermined**. Non-peer-reviewed industry investigation; image sizing can depend on photographic alignment. Do not convert geometry measurements to a universal grind, dose, taste or extraction correction.
3. **DECENT-DE1-MANUAL-CURRENT**, manufacturer first-party maintained manual, https://decentespresso.com/docs/de1_manual , §§1.9, 4.1, full public text read Nov 19. **High for stated DE1 user-facing pressure/flow profile features; not independent sensory efficacy.** In pressure mode the machine holds a pressure target while flow responds to puck; in flow mode it attempts flow target subject to pressure limits. Manual documents that flow=0 with exhaust closed differs hydraulically from setting pressure=0 which opens release valve. The manual itself flags 2026 software/model changes, so do not infer identical behavior across DE1/Bengle/third-party app versions. Manufacturer control capabilities do **not** prove that uniform puck extraction or improved cup quality results.

## Explicit claim verification and uncertainty
- **R371-BASKET-01 / OBSERVED in narrow comparative setup:** high-flow basket was associated with higher reported EY **after redialing finer**, while dose nearly fixed but yield/time also differed. Confounded comparison; practical observational evidence, not one-knob randomized efficacy. Status **BOUNDED / NO app numeric policy change**.
- **R371-BASKET-02 / MECHANISM UNVERIFIED:** basket-hole count or % open area alone does **not** determine espresso flow/extraction. Flow is dominated by coupled puck/basket and potential clogging/edge effects; source author explicitly leaves exact geometry mechanism open. Do not manufacture a hole-area-to-grind coefficient, universal EY or “taste better” rule.
- **R371-CONTROL-03 / MANUFACTURER-DOCUMENTED:** DE1 pressure vs closed-loop flow are distinct controllable machine modes, not interchangeable with passive E61 paddle. Neither mode can infer spatial puck uniformity from aggregate outflow, and manufacturer copy is not a blinded sensory result. Current DIC does not capture/validate pressure-curve interventions; **do not invent them or propose automatic machine-specific corrections**.
