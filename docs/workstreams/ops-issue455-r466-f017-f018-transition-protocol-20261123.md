## Research-only optional/consented future evaluation specification (NO implementation or data read)
**Question:** On the *same* coffee release, user, setup/grinder calibration/version, basket, style and feed mode, does a **single actually made** proposed controllable adjustment predict a better next fully rated shot than the user's prior state — and how often does it worsen the experience?

**Eligibility and provenance, in chronological order:**
1. Only after a separately approved, informed and revocable opt-in, define a short per-coffee dial-in session; preserve private identifiers without public disclosure, do not backfill private historical user data without permission. Bind `before_shot_id`, its immutable measured dose/yield/time/grind/optional temperature, all five raw taste axes and contemporaneous recipe context.
2. Record `offer_id`, immutable exact **current approved advice text/suggested single control/target**, source algorithm version, whether shown and whether an owner freely chose to try it. A clicked Use Recommendation is **not** acceptance; distinguish displayed, copied, self-reported used, actually measured, rejected, unknown and never shown. Do not infer accepted action solely from target equality.
3. Bind exactly one chronological later `next_shot_id` in same coffee release + same owner/setup version/calibration/basket/style/feed + actual matched controlled change; reject ambiguous chronology, cross-device stale snapshots, unsupported grinder translations, changes to dose/yield/grind/temp beyond the intended one, altered puck prep/pressure/time definition, uncertain coffee identity or substantial rest/age change from **causal-eligibility** analysis. Keep those records in a separate descriptive/confounder bucket if appropriately consented; never delete original History. Measured time is a response/outcome, not necessarily independently controlled.
4. Preserve **all five** ordinal ratings before and after, their completion flags and unchanged definitions. Mark `all_green_next` only for complete five ideals and physically valid entry, distinct from prior-green, invalid import, different valid alternate family and unrated. Report a five-component signed ordinal change vector; avoid inventing a psychometric sum or pretending 1–5 spacing equals interval distance. Define *worsening* conservatively as any new/regressed severe dryness, sour/bitter/extraction imbalance, body mismatch, lost sweetness/clarity, choke/stall or other owner-reported adverse result; these are separate measured outcomes, not automatic algorithmic corrections.
5. Denominators must include eligible offers, no action taken, absent next shots, missing/incomplete ratings and unobserved outcomes as separately labeled categories. Never drop unfavorable/unfinished outcomes from a claimed effectiveness rate; report missingness and selection. Store no new telemetry, member data, action flags, device, location, private notes, raw identifiers or extra permissions without separate owner approval and RLS/retention design.
6. Report descriptive paired proportions for exact green, per-dimension ordinal better/same/worse, attempts to first green, worsening/stall rate, return/abstention and missingness; denominators and uncertainty intervals by sufficiently supported setup/coffee/style/feed subgroup. Use subject/coffee/session clustered sensitivity intervals and chronological holdouts; avoid reuse of later outcomes to select earlier recipes. Small samples or no overlap must trigger **ABSTAIN / NOT ESTIMABLE** rather than impressive probability labels. Without randomized, authorized policy allocation or suitable identifiability assumptions this cannot establish **causal improvement**.
7. Protect against confounding by bean age/rest, water, basket, puck preparation, grind/burr wear, machine heating/pressure, target beverage mass, dose, temperature measurement, parallel human advice, order/learning, natural shot-to-shot variance, taste drift, selective rating, offline delayed sync and multiple family selection. In a deliberately separate future owner-approved design, ethical controls and privacy/security review would precede any prospective intervention. No experiment or treatment allocation here.

**Negative and positive acceptance examples (PROPOSED, NOT TEST EXECUTIONS):** (a) same context and one correctly measured change + all five before/after ratings is potentially *descriptive eligible*; (b) copied starting-reference button without actual matching change is NOT accepted next-shot advice; (c) an unchanged setting/no next shot has no efficacy event; (d) changing grind and yield together, or toggling Slow Feed, is confounded and excluded from single-change efficacy while retained descriptively; (e) missing fifth rating cannot count green; (f) one worse dryness or stalled flow is adverse, not silently filtered; (g) a different coffee release/version, incompatible calibration or archived Custom style is not direct transferable evidence; (h) current all-green success is success even if style time is atypical, subject to valid physical measurements.
