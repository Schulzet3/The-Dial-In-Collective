## R424 bounded independent original-source read

- **Primary practitioner analysis:** Jonathan Gagné, [What I Learned From Analyzing 300 Particle Size Distributions for 24 Espresso Grinders](https://coffeeadastra.com/2023/09/21/what-i-learned-from-analyzing-300-particle-size-distributions-for-24-espresso-grinders/), September 21, 2023. Directly read author original HTML, including sample/equipment, reference normalization, plotted associations and explicit limitations. Gagné examined approximately 300 PSD observations across 24 grinders using samples and Camsizer X2 measurement from the Kaffeemacher/ZHAW comparison. A single natural Brazilian coffee was the underlying PSD comparison; results were normalized using a 340-micrometre reference median (selected to lie in overlapping grinder ranges), with fitted curves, not a randomized grinder-geometry intervention. Within those observations, conical groups averaged more fines/less uniformity but overlapped flat groups; RPM lacked a clear global association; no monotonic diameter–uniformity relationship appeared in the tested mostly 50–70 mm flats plus one 83 mm flat. Burr geometry, alignment, feed and RPM were not independently controlled; no causal burr-label effect, universally transferable calibration, or validated sensory outcome follows.
- **Related original tasting-study provenance:** Benjamin Hohlmann and Kaffeemacher, [Espressomühlen für Zuhause](https://kaffeemacher.ch/blogs/kaffeewissen/espressomuehlen-zuhause), dated March 12, 2023, direct first-party German HTML. Their broader staged comparison describes 30 grinders across the project, with the public 24-grinder early comparison and further nine finalists; hence a 24-PSD subset must not be conflated with all 30 grinders. Limited expert sensory assessment of selected finalists used separately dialed-in recipes and coffee choices; it is **not** an isolated grinder-shape taste trial, DIC five-scale member ratings, or single-setting shot-to-next-shot outcome.
- **Evidence grade/status:** firsthand practitioner-measured **observational PSD / methods directly reviewed**, limited coffee, sample, PSD metrics, equipment states and replication; useful mechanistic context, **not independent causal burr-class proof**. Underlying 300 raw PSD records, plotted point-by-point values, alternative-model sensitivity, instrument calibration, full tasting data and external replication were **NOT independently reproduced/verified**. No statistical significance or science-confidence percentage is inferred. Claims about espresso cup quality, recommended personal grind numbers, universally optimal RPM/burr diameter or next-shot gain remain **UNSUPPORTED** by these sources.
