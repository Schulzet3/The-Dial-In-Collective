## Primary-paper verification: pressure saturation is a long-time apparatus finding, NOT a next-shot dial-in rule

**R386-06-POROELASTIC-HORIZON (direct first-party author paper, experimental-specific evidence B; universal/espresso-taste evidence D).** Directly read Waszkiewicz et al., *Under pressure: poroelastic regulation of flow in espresso brewing*, *Physics of Fluids* 38, 063113 (2026), DOI [10.1063/5.0319611](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0319611), author HTML [arXiv:2512.21528v2, 9 May 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.21528v2), §§II.1–II.5, III, IV, V and VI. This is **the original authors' text**, not solely the June 23 AIP press release. Experiment used one Brazilian light-to-medium-light coffee, fixed `18.50 +/- 0.05 g` dose, Fiorenzato F64 EVO setting 1.9, Sanremo Zoe Competition, IMS 58 mm basket, controlled puck preparation and pressure-path calibration. **Sixty coffees over eleven basket-pressure conditions** were deliberately brewed **at least 120 s**; the fitted nonlinear/saturating pressure–flow curve in Fig. 6 used the **last 10 s** of those runs (roughly 100+ s), not independently measured sensory optima in 20–40 s normal shots. Low-pressure (<5 bar) approximately Darcy-like behavior and high-pressure *long-time* saturation are supported for this apparatus, not universal pressure-versus-yield or pressure-versus-flavor prescriptions. Authors' fitted `Q_c=1.90 +/- 0.15 g/s`, `P_c=12 +/- 3 bar` are **study calibration scales, not DIC targets**.
 
**R386-06-TRANSIENT-UNCERTAINTY (model/fraction evidence B within study; generalization D).** Earlier TDS course was sampled in 5-second fractions with **three repeated fraction-series** and a VST refractometer; it is separate from sixty long-time pressure sweeps. The coarse-grained dynamic model qualitatively represents dissolution/flow feedback but expressly **does not resolve early multiphase air displacement, wetting/swelling, spatial heterogeneity, fines migration or channel formation** (authors' §V and concluding limitations). The paper's "majority of readily extractable compounds" language does **not** mean all coffee solubles dissolve within 30–40 s, and its equation's normalized "universal shape" is a model statement conditional on selected constitutive laws, not a universal experimentally validated espresso taste law. Data do not demonstrate any five-ordinal-taste or personalized next-shot quality outcome. The press-release simplification about "all the coffee dissolves" must not become a factual app claim.
