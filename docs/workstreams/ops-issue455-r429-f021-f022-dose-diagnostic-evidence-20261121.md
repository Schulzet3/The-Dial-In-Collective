**F021 — hold dose / change one controllable setting for interpretability.** Original creator [Hoffmann dose episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aTFsBqhpLes), [Hoffmann dialing episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lFwJF-_SUr0) and [Hedrick approach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bk-Dvml5Vrg) were identified at original URLs; creator video descriptions/transcripts and every demonstrated outcome were **not** independently available in full in this run, so grade **expert-practice support, limited direct verification**. Choosing a consistent basket-appropriate dose and changing one control at a time is sensible experimental design, but no controlled magnitude or causal improvement in five DIC taste scores was established. A user can deliberately change their dose; this does not become a new prohibition. Existing Locked app guidance takes precedence over any unverified research pack recommendation.

**F022 — dryness/astringency cannot uniquely identify over-extraction or channeling.** Primary 2020 [Cameron et al. *Matter* DOI 10.1016/j.matt.2019.12.019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matt.2019.12.019), checked against original journal-linked and [PNNL institutional abstract](https://www.pnnl.gov/publications/systematically-improving-espresso-insights-mathematical-modeling-and-experiment), reports nonmonotonic extraction yield and suggests inhomogeneous flow with finer grinding; this is not a validation of an astringency classifier. Primary [Gagné 2021 puck-resistance experiment](https://coffeeadastra.com/2021/01/16/a-study-of-espresso-puck-resistance-and-how-puck-preparation-affects-it/) reports 24 one-setup shots with changing surface/deep WDT and paper: the author explicitly did **not** refract the shots, and did not establish an isolated link from dryness scores to channeling or a universally superior preparation method. S012 [Hoffmann troubleshooting creator video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MbTD42FvMVU) has source identity but no fully verified transcript or sensory experiment here. **Evidence grade for the exact dryness→uneven-flow mechanism is C / indirect plausible alternative, not a measured diagnostic.** The appropriate claim is uncertainty/abstention, not a causal cure or a numeric grind/temperature command.
