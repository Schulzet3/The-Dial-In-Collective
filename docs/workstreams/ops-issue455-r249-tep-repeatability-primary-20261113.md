**Claim checks (new sidecar IDs pending canonical source ledger reconciliation):**

- `R455-EV-CIT-249-A` / **protocol mismatch; DIRECT PUBLISHER FULL METHODS, HIGH confidence for what the authors did, NO DIC efficacy inference**. The panel used 46 coffee-industry experts, each with >=3 years experience: France 7, India 12, Italy 7, Republic of Korea 10, USA 10. Eight distinct coffee samples (seven green origins roasted locally plus prepackaged already-roasted), **three replications per expert**, using an expert questionnaire of **CATA sensory descriptors, two 0–100 quality scales, check-one/yes-no questions and free text** on personal mobile devices. Participants were provided a coffee lexicon but *not separately trained on this study's descriptors*. This is **not** the application's five mandatory personal 1–5 tastes, nor a general-population comparison.
- `R455-EV-CIT-249-B` / **repeatability statistics; DIRECT FIRST-PARTY ORIGINAL RESULTS TEXT, bounded evidence**. CLUSCATA within-person descriptor clustering was repeatable in **9/10 USA, 5/7 Italy, 10/10 Korea, 9/12 India, 6/7 France**; global homogeneity index **0.364 for n=138 participant-replications**, and country-specific index >=0.5 in four countries except Italy. Correspondence-analysis RV comparisons were statistically significant in **five of ten** pairings, not ten of ten. Repeatable selection of categorical descriptors by selected experts is not a calibrated confidence level for personal espresso recipe advice. Exact underlying Table 6–9 cells and independent reproduction **not inspected**; refrain from claiming fully reproduced statistics.
- `R455-EV-CIT-249-C` / **design confounding; DIRECT ORIGINAL METHODS/LIMITATIONS, factual conditions high confidence, causal inference limited**. Countries did **not** standardize roast level: a local professional chose it for each green coffee. India rested samples **20 days** instead of planned **3–10**; local commercial grinders, brewing details and water conditions differed. Each country served **8–16** samples per session across **2–3 days**, a possible tasting-fatigue influence acknowledged by the authors. Different cultural terminology and unequal per-country sample sizes can confound whether between-country descriptors reflect culture rather than preparation or protocol learning. The study is not an equipment-constant, same-coffee, one-controlled-change, next-shot intervention.
- `R455-EV-CIT-249-D` / **translation to product and privacy; ORIGINAL RESULTS/LIMITATIONS/ETHICS plus pinned LIVE code**. The authors themselves call for sensory-lexicon training and future tests with **consumers**; the present study never tests the app, its 1–5 sensory inputs or any individualized one-variable recommendation. IRB and consent were recorded for participants, and the datasets were restricted for privacy; this **does not authorize** reading DIC member records, collecting telemetry or conducting product experiments. The two study-specific brewing dose/serving quantities are **not** scientifically established DIC universal optima. Grade: direct authentic primary article for methodology and reported results; **no observed benefit or policy authority for DIC recommendation effectiveness**.
