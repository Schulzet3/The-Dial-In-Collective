2. **Measured association only:** printed Table II Pearson r with subjective overall satisfaction: richness **0.31**, aroma **0.20**, bitterness **-0.16**, perceived concentration **0.10** (`R455-EV-279-B`). These are not taste-intervention effects; Table II has two ambiguously labeled p-value rows; no independent raw data, confidence intervals or adjusted regression review. Do not transform correlations into scientifically confident guidance.
