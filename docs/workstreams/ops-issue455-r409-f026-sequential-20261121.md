## Original finding and independently checked primary sources
- **F026 original** (pack `findings.json`, original member SHA-256 `ac76010a1137c18b…`): successive contradictory observations can prompt reversal of a *hypothesis*. Original tags **application_heuristic_requiring_validation**, **C**, **experimental**; `numerical_effect=null`, no hazard threshold, no prospective espresso study. “Second worse shot cancels earlier finer suggestion” is an **illustrative proposed test**, not approved behavior or a validated causal result. Pack's implementation proposals are not policy.
- **S002 Cameron et al.**, *Matter* **2**(3), 631–648, published March 4, 2020, DOI `10.1016/j.matt.2019.12.019` ([full publisher text](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matt.2019.12.019), [PNNL abstract](https://www.pnnl.gov/publications/systematically-improving-espresso-insights-mathematical-modeling-and-experiment)). In one studied extraction system, yield peaked rather than increasing monotonically as grind became finer; fine grind could give inhomogeneous extraction and poor reproducibility. The paper explicitly notes that flavor differences were not quantified and tasting remains necessary. **Grade A for physical extraction conditional on experimental setup; NOT evidence that reacting to a single worsening all-five subjective rating with a reversal improves the next cup (grade D / unverified for that outcome).** No universal numerical seconds/click hazard cutoff.
- **S007 Lance Hedrick original creator video** ([2025-04-16 creator listing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nqsdYO0PPIU)). Independently observed creator description: a small move finer causing a much longer shot may signal a practical grind boundary and motivate coarser dialing. This is **practitioner heuristic grade B**, not a blinded sequential next-shot taste reversal test, verified transcript or universal threshold. The original `S018` is a **2026-10-29 code snapshot**, not independent peer-reviewed science; use current Git blobs for behavior instead.
- Combined factual finding: **conditional physical nonlinearity** and **practical advice to reconsider an unsuccessful adjustment**; no proof that worsening taste was caused by the prior recommendation. Confounders include evolving coffee age, grinder drift/calibration, dosing, puck preparation, water/temperature, basket/feed mode, extraction time and intra-taster variability. Directional learning, automated action reversal or quantified success claims remain **UNVERIFIED**.
